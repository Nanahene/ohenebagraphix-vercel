The Valentine's Diary was an independent creative project built around personal expression and keeping meaningful memories. Rather than a one-day gift, it was designed as a space where people could write, reflect, and hold onto their own memories long after Valentine's Day had passed.

Every diary was personalized for its recipient, with the inner pages carrying their name and a dedication written just for them, so each copy felt like it belonged to the person holding it rather than a generic gift.

The diary itself doesn't try to tell the reader's story. It's meant to give them somewhere to write it.

The project brought together the design of the cover, inner pages, and personalized details into one cohesive, Valentine-appropriate identity, then carried that through to a physical product: 20 copies were printed and shared freely with family, friends, and anyone who showed interest.
